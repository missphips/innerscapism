What is Innerscapism?

Innerscapism is a practice of returning.

To your attention.
To your curiosity.
To your body.
To place.
To each other.
To the parts of yourself that become quieter when life gets busy.

We spend so much of our lives doing, solving, producing and responding. Innerscapism creates space to pause, notice, make and ask better questions.

Creative practices are the medium, not the goal.
No artistic skill required. Just curiosity.
