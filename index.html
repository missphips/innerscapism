About

Held by someone
who has walked the path too.

Innerscapism is a practice of returning.
To the body, to place, to the parts of yourself that got quiet during loss or burnout. Sessions blend simple art-making (collage, texture transfer, mark-making) with reflective conversation, no artistic skill required.

Every practice is an invitation, never a performance.
You bring your hands and your attention, the rest unfolds.

How it works

Two ways to begin

Workshops for teams

On-site or remote sessions for teams navigating change, burnout risk, or collective grief. Shared art-making opens honest conversation that meetings can't reach. Fostering real connection and psychological safety.

  • 60–120 minute sessions
  • Groups of 6–30
  • Materials provided

1:1 online

Individual sessions from wherever you are.
A quiet, private space to process grief, burnout or transition at your own pace, over video. 
Let's meet.

  • 50-minute sessions
  • Weekly or as-needed
  • Free discovery call first
