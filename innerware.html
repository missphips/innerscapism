Creating technology from the inside out.

We are building technologies that can write, create, recommend, analyse and decide faster than ever.

But the question isn't only what technology can do.
It's what technology is doing to us.

The shift

The more powerful our tools become, the more important our inner capacities become.

AI is changing how we work, create, communicate and make decisions. As more of our cognitive work becomes automated, we need to become more conscious of the things technology cannot decide for us:

What matters.
What is meaningful.
What deserves our attention.
What should remain human.

Innerware explores this space between human inner life and the systems we create.

A Zen story

The horse is getting faster.

There is a story in Zen circles about a man and a horse.

A man is riding a horse that is galloping very quickly. It appears that the man is going somewhere very important.

Another man, standing alongside the road, calls out:

“Where are you going?”

The rider shouts back:

“I don't know! Ask the horse!”

The horse continues to run faster and faster. The man on the road calls:

“Then stop the horse!”

And the rider answers:

“I don't know how!”

If this story already sounds familiar when applied to human behaviour, imagine it now in the age of AI.

We have built a horse that can run faster than we ever could.

It can write. Design. Code. Analyse. Generate. Recommend.

And our response has largely been:

Faster.More.Automate it.Optimise it.Scale it.

But somewhere in this race, we may have forgotten to ask:

Where are we actually going?

Acceleration is not the same as direction.

Who is holding the reins?

What is Innerware?

Innerware is a practice of designing from the inside out.

Innerware explores the relationship between inner life and technology. It brings together:

  • Product thinking
  • UX
  • AI
  • Creativity
  • Embodiment
  • Humanistic design
  • Attention
  • Agency
  • Systems thinking

The purpose isn't to reject technology.

It is to become more conscious of the relationship we have with it, and the kinds of relationships our products create for others.

Four areas

Where we look.

01Attention

What is your product teaching people to pay attention to?

Exploring how interfaces, notifications, algorithms and interactions shape attention.

02Agency

Are we helping people make better decisions, or making decisions for them?

Exploring autonomy, delegation, AI and human choice.

03Creativity

What happens to creativity when generation becomes effortless?

Exploring the relationship between AI, originality, expression and creative agency.

04Humanity

What should remain human?

Exploring meaning, uncertainty, intuition, connection and the things we shouldn't optimise away.

From inner to outer

We go inward to create differently outward.

Innerscapism begins with the inner world. But the inner world doesn't exist separately from the world we build.

The way we understand ourselves shapes the way we relate to others.

The way we relate to others shapes what we create.

And what we create becomes part of the systems that shape everyone else.

From inner landscape to outer world.
